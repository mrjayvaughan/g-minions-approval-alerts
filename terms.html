Terms of Service

These terms govern the private G & Minions Codex Alerts SMS service.

1. Service scope

The service sends low-volume operational SMS alerts to one phone number owned and controlled by the registered sole proprietor. An alert indicates that a local Codex desktop agent may be waiting for approval. The service is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

2. Consent and enrollment

The operator enrolls by texting ALERTS to (320) 373-2666, reviewing the disclosure message, and replying Y to confirm. Enrollment remains inactive until final confirmation. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

3. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with agent activity, up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply according to the operator's wireless plan.

4. Opt-out and support

Reply STOP to end alerts. Reply START to re-enable messages after opting out. Reply HELP for SMS support and service information.

5. Safety boundaries

SMS alerts are informational only. They do not include approval links and cannot approve or deny an agent action. The operator must review and decide every approval inside the Codex application. The service must not be used for marketing, third-party messaging, emergencies, or transmission of sensitive data.

6. Availability

Delivery is not guaranteed. Wireless carriers, Twilio, the local computer, network availability, and software state may delay or prevent a message. The service is not an emergency notification system.

The operator may pause or discontinue the service at any time. Material changes to these terms will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For support and service information, reply HELP by SMS to (320) 373-2666.
