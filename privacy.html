Privacy Policy

This service is designed to disclose as little as possible while delivering one-recipient operational alerts.

1. Information processed

The service processes the enrolled mobile number, opt-in and opt-out status, inbound enrollment keywords, message timestamps, and delivery status. It does not intentionally collect prompts, commands, filenames, customer records, approval details, or message replies beyond the enrollment and HELP/STOP workflow.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to confirm consent, send Codex approval-waiting alerts, provide support, prevent duplicate alerts, enforce a message cap, and maintain basic delivery records.

3. Sharing

Mobile-number and message-routing data is processed by Twilio and participating wireless carriers to deliver SMS messages. Information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

4. Retention

Consent status is retained while alerts remain enabled. Minimal delivery logs may be retained temporarily for troubleshooting and duplicate prevention, then deleted when no longer needed. Carrier and Twilio records are governed by their own retention practices.

5. Security

Twilio credentials remain outside message content and are stored locally by the operator. Alerts use fixed templates and exclude secrets or approval details. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

6. Choices

Reply STOP to withdraw SMS consent at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. Because this is a one-recipient service, requests to review or delete locally retained information can be made directly to the operator.

7. Children

The service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's information.

8. Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. For support and privacy questions, reply HELP by SMS to (320) 373-2666.
