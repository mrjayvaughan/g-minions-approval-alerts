Know when your agent needs you.

G & Minions Codex Alerts is a one-recipient SMS service that notifies its registered operator when a local Codex desktop agent is waiting for approval.

G & Minionsnow
Codex needs your approval. Open the Codex app to review. SMS replies cannot approve or deny this request.

No commands, secrets, customer data, or approval links are included.

Explicit double opt-in

Enrollment takes two replies.

Enrollment is limited to one phone number owned and controlled by the registered operator. The service does not accept third-party subscribers.

1

Request enrollment

Text ALERTS to (320) 373-2666.

2

Review the disclosure

You will receive a welcome message describing the service, message frequency, rates, and HELP/STOP options.

3

Confirm enrollment

Reply Y to confirm. Alerts remain disabled until this final confirmation is received.

Disclosure: Up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

What alerts contain

Minimal by design.

  • A short notice that Codex is waiting for approval.
  • A reminder to review the request inside the Codex app.
  • Standard HELP and STOP instructions when required.

What alerts cannot do

No remote approval.

  • No SMS reply can approve or deny an agent action.
  • No prompt, command, filename, secret, or customer data is sent.
  • No marketing or third-party messaging is permitted.
